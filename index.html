What I've built

Hackathon project, team of three

What I'm exploring next

Offline app backup and cloning

Early concept, for places where mobile data is expensive

Reinstalling apps and re-downloading everything costs real money in Nigeria and India. This tool would back up and restore apps without spending data. The hard part, and the part I think is worth building, is working out how to restore each app properly.

  • Offline sharing: send app installers between phones over WiFi Direct or Bluetooth.
  • Smart backup: save only what matters, like media, documents and game progress.
  • Restore assistant: walk you through reinstalling and getting each app back to how it was.
  • Data saver mode: show how much data you saved.
